Her Beginnings (trigger warning: animal abuse, animal death, and sad realities) (All these stories come to me 2nd hand through an ex I never speak to anymore, so if they are inaccurate and you know the true story, please take them with a grain of giant salt) 
</p>
<br>
<p>Kittles was born on an icy day. To a mother who was known to wander about in Tom's ex-wife Karis' parents' backyard. She gave birth to a litter of kittens in an abandoned doghouse. Small mews carried through the air, permeating Tom's attentive ears. Kittens, he thought, and rushed to the backyard and found the litter in distress. 
Most of the kittens were in awful shape, some cold and stiff and having passed on; three survived, and Tom brought them inside. Wishing to see them safe and sound. Kittles' eyes were sealed shut with gunk. She was the runt. She had been on the bottom of the pile. One of the other kittens didn't make it through the night, but Kittles did. Tom cuddled and cleaned her, then wrapped her in warm towels, trying to bring her back to health and safety.
Poor Kittles had terrible infections in her eyes, and every day needed them washed and taken care of to help her see. But soon she was a mewling ball of affection. Happy to see her sibling and her older actual Aunt. Cheap Cheap. </P>
<br>
<p>One day, a horrible dog, the demon of the home, came growling into the bedroom where Kittles and her sibling and Cheap Cheap were cuddled up warm and purring. This wretched creature, underfed and abused by Keri's sibling, was hungry, and the kittens looked like a delicious treat. 
He attacked the cats, and Cheap Cheap grabbed Kittles by the scruff and dragged the tiniest kitten up into the rafters away from the dog, but was too late to save her sibling. From that day on, Kittles was terrified of dogs.</P>
<br>
<p>Let us not forget that Kittles' original name was actually Midna. But anyone who knows Kittles knows that she is no Midna. Others started calling her 'little one' or 'little Kitty', and over time that was shortened to 'Kittles'.</P>
<br>
<p>Kittles was an unwanted kitten. Tom and his ex were both overrun with other cats. It was the 2008 financial crisis, and they were both suffering from unemployment and living with family, and couldn't afford more cats. Plus Keri found Kittles to be 'stupid' and would throw her away when she tried to cuddle and even kick the loving cat.
So Tom tried to find her a home. First, he tried with his Mother, who found her not a great cat; she didn't bond with her and tried to find her a home with two other families. By the time Tom had gotten kittles back, she was a unfriendly cat. Terrified of feet and brooms and strangers. Wishing to be left alone and hate loafing.</P>
