<p>
Kittles was deep in her hatred of humanity when A’ledyn came along. He was visiting Oregon to see some friends and met Tom while at an arcade. Al went over to Tom’s home to meet his kitties, but mainly because he was rather taken with the slim man’s energy. Tom brought Kittles up the stairs of the three-story townhouse where he lived to meet the trio of strangers.</p>
<br>
<p>
The small calico’s big eyes were wide with fright as she was carried. Claws sinking into Tom’s shoulder. As soon as he set her down, she scurried to escape and ran into a scary lady, whom she hissed at. She ran from the other man in the room right into Al’s lap, where she hid her head. </p>
<p>
<br>
Al was taken with the calico and gave her pets and cooed, and in that moment fell in love with her. But the love was far from returned. </p>
<p>
<br>
Months passed, and Al moved to Oregon to be with Tom. Tom was still living with some of his friends, in their basement with Cheap, Cheap, and Kittles. Cheebers hid under the bed, and Kittles was a perpetual loaf of hatred on the double-sided velvet blanket of the bed. Al had no job yet, and while Tom was at work, spent many hours lying next to Kittles in her hatred. Sometimes staring at the very wall the cat was staring at. </p>
<p>
<br>
Kittles would sometimes hiss and attack Al, and Al just let her. Not really caring about the pain. Loving the time with the little Cat. Holding her close even when she was upset, until the screaming was gone. </p>
<p>
<br>
Thus their friendship began. Kittles and Al were both in a small space, together. Their bond slowly grew.</p>
